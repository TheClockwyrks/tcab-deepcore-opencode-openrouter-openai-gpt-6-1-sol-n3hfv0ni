# Deepcore

**Depth is cheap. The way back costs everything.** You are the last prospector on a silent mining world. Above you: a scrapped camp and a rocket that does not work. Below you: a planet full of things worth bringing home.

Cut a shaft through rust-colored earth, follow a promising vein, and fill your bay. Then make the climb. Every kilogram slows your jetpack, every second takes fuel, and every extra tile is a decision you will have to live with on the way back. Sell your haul, refill your reserves, and build a suit that can take you deeper.

Gray rock gives way to cold blue stone and a glowing molten shell. Watch for the faint breath of a gas pocket. Brake before a hard landing. A scanner can lead you to the crystals your escape rocket needs, but the final prize is different: an unstable Core Sample, and ninety seconds to get it home.

Choose your world size and your stakes. Standard lets you fall back on a Save Pad checkpoint; Hardcore makes a fatal mistake final. Five rocket systems stand between you and the sky. Bring back enough to build them, and leave this rock behind.
